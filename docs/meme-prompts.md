# CHASS meme prompts (for Antigravity image generation)

Chess-piece parodies of famous meme formats. Each one redraws the joke with our own characters. Do not trace, copy or name the original meme in the prompt, and keep real people, logos and existing cartoon characters out.

## Instructions for Antigravity

1. Read the style block below and attach these references to every generation:
   - `reference/user-supplied/chad-horse-sketch.jpg` (the Chad Horse knight, our flagship character)
   - `public/piecesets/chad/wn.svg` and `public/piecesets/chad/wn-smug.svg` (our knight art)
2. Generate each prompt below as a square image, 1024 x 1024, 2 to 4 variations each.
3. Save every variation as PNG to `reference/memes-raw/<id>-<n>.png`, for example `reference/memes-raw/distracted-pawn-1.png`.
4. No text, letters, numbers, speech bubbles, watermarks or signatures in any image. Captions are added by the game.

## Style block (prepend to every prompt)

Bold flat cartoon illustration, thick dark outlines, simple cel shading, warm saturated colours, expressive exaggerated faces. Characters are anthropomorphic chess pieces with faces, eyes, eyebrows and small arms; white pieces are ivory, black pieces are charcoal. The knight is a smug, muscular, bearded horse in the style of the attached reference. Clean uncluttered background with hints of a green and cream chessboard (#769656 and #EEEED2) and gold accents (#F1C40F). Centered composition that still reads when shrunk to 100 pixels. Absolutely no text or lettering anywhere.

## Prompts

### 1. distracted-pawn
A white pawn walking hand in hand with its white queen suddenly turns its head to stare at a glamorous black pawn walking past. The white queen glares at the pawn in outrage. Street scene made of chessboard tiles.
- Game caption ideas: "free pawn?" / "eyes on the board"
- Moments: poisoned_bait, free_pawn

### 2. pained-smile-knight
The Chad Horse knight sits at a desk with a laptop showing a chessboard, holding a coffee mug and forcing a very strained polite smile, eyes full of quiet pain. Behind him his own queen is being carried away.
- Game caption ideas: "it's fine. I meant that." / "all part of the plan"
- Moments: hung_queen, blunder

### 3. trade-offer-knight
The Chad Horse knight in a sharp suit and tie stands centered, facing the viewer with a confident deal-making pose, hands spread apart: one hand holds out a tiny pawn, the other hand gestures toward a big shiny queen. Plain room background.
- Game caption ideas: "trade offer: I get your queen, you get a pawn"
- Moments: queen_trade, exchange_sac

### 4. two-buttons-bishop
A sweating black bishop wipes its forehead with a cloth, panicking while looking at two big red buttons on a control panel in front of it. Close-up of the bishop's anxious face and the two buttons.
- Game caption ideas: "take the pawn / save the queen"
- Moments: long_think, time_scramble

### 5. this-is-fine-rook
A white rook sits calmly at a small table sipping tea, smiling, while the chessboard room around it is fully on fire. Cartoon flames, the rook is relaxed.
- Game caption ideas: "this position is fine"
- Moments: losing_position, dead_lost

### 6. approve-reject-king
Two stacked panels. Top: a white king turns away with one hand raised in disgust, refusing a neat castle tower. Bottom: the same king points and smiles in approval at a lonely open road leading into the middle of the board.
- Game caption ideas: "castling / king walk"
- Moments: early_king_walk

### 7. galaxy-brain-pawn
Four stacked panels of a pawn's head with its brain growing: small dim brain, normal brain, glowing brain, then a cosmic glowing brain radiating light across the galaxy.
- Game caption ideas: "move a piece / develop / sacrifice / sacrifice the queen"
- Moments: brilliant_move, absurd_sac

### 8. yelling-queen-smug-horse
Two-panel scene. Left: an angry crying white queen pointing and yelling, held back by a friend pawn. Right: the Chad Horse knight sitting at a dinner table in front of a plate of salad, looking confused and smug.
- Game caption ideas: "you're using an engine / I just play good"
- Moments: engine_vibes, brilliant_move

### 9. shocked-pawn
Close-up of a pawn with a wide-open mouth and huge round eyes, frozen in shock, hands on cheeks. Simple light background.
- Game caption ideas: "it was defended?"
- Moments: blunder, walked_into

### 10. buff-knight-tiny-knight
Side-by-side. Left: an enormous muscular Chad Horse knight standing proudly. Right: a tiny sad skinny knight sitting and crying.
- Game caption ideas: "knights in 1990 / knights in my games"
- Moments: steamroll, comeback

### 11. clown-bishop
Four stacked panels of a bishop at a mirror putting on clown makeup, one step per panel: foundation, red nose, colourful wig, full clown face.
- Game caption ideas: "winning / +5 / one move / stalemate"
- Moments: throw, stalemate_from_winning

### 12. stonks-pawn
A pawn in a business suit stands proudly in front of a giant rising green chart arrow, with a glowing city skyline made of chess pieces behind it.
- Game caption ideas: "material up"
- Moments: free_pawn, capture_spree

### 13. side-eye-king
A white king looks awkwardly away to the side with a nervous side-eye, pretending not to notice something happening off-screen. Tight crop on the face.
- Game caption ideas: "I didn't see anything"
- Moments: opponent_blunder

### 14. is-this-a-queen-pawn
A confused pawn points with one hand at a yellow rubber duck sitting on the chessboard, looking at the viewer with a puzzled face, as if asking whether it is a queen.
- Game caption ideas: "is this a queen?"
- Moments: duck_chess, underpromotion

### 15. panic-calm-panic-king
Three stacked panels of a king. Top: panicking, sweating, eyes wide. Middle: calm and relieved, smiling. Bottom: panicking even harder.
- Game caption ideas: "check / blocked it / check again"
- Moments: check_spam, escape_then_check
