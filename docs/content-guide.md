# Adding content (no code changes)

Everything below lives in `public/` and is loaded at runtime. A bad file shows a red
"Content problem" banner in the app instead of crashing it.

## A new capture cinematic: `public/cinematics/cinematics.json`

```json
{
  "id": "rook-bonks-bishop",
  "match": { "attacker": "r", "victim": "b", "variant": "*" },
  "weight": 2,
  "duration": 1500,
  "steps": [
    { "at": 0,   "do": "spawn",   "id": "bonk", "sprite": "bonk.svg", "anchor": "victim", "size": 1.4 },
    { "at": 200, "do": "caption", "text": ["bonk", "go to horny jail"], "ms": 900 },
    { "at": 200, "do": "shake",   "target": "victim", "ms": 400 },
    { "at": 300, "do": "fade",    "target": "victim", "to": 0, "ms": 600 }
  ]
}
```

- Match: `attacker` and `victim` take `p n b r q k d`, a list, or `*`. Optional `attackerColor`, `victimColor` (`w`/`b`) and `variant` (id or list: `fischer960`, `dragonBishop`, `duck`).
- The most specific match wins (attacker +4, victim +2, variant +1). Several entries in the same tier are picked by `weight`. An entry with `"match": {}` is the fallback.
- Steps: `spawn`, `aim`, `move`, `rotate`, `scale`, `caption`, `shake`, `fade`, `pose`, `sound`, `despawn`. `at` is ms from the start. `target` is `attacker`, `victim`, `sq1`..`sq4` (moment gags) or a spawned `id`.
- `aim` puts a gun hand on the board with the aim-mode geometry: `{ "do": "aim", "id": "h", "from": "sq1", "to": "sq2", "ms": 900 }` slides a little way out of `from` and points at `to` (the hand never travels to the target), holds `ms` and fades. It plays no sound (add `reload`). A gag with an `aim` counts as a gun gag.
- `caption` takes `pos` (`top`, `center`, `bottom`; default bottom) and `style` (a CSS class on `.cine-caption`; shipped: `small`, `red`).
- Anchors: `attacker`, `victim`, `from`, `to`, `between`, `sq1`..`sq4` (a moment's squares, see below), a square like `e4`, or `aim:<reach>` (the gun's spot: a little in front of the attacker on the line to the victim, and at least `reach` squares short of the victim; a hand never travels across the board). `rotate: "toTarget"` aims a sprite that faces right at the victim (mirrored when it points left).
- Sprites live in `public/sprites/`. Sounds: `sfx` is a built-in name (`bang`, `shot`, `reload`, `capture`, `move`, `castle`, `duck`, `check`, `promote`, `mate`, `draw`, `blunder`, `dice`, `meme`) or a file under `public/sounds/`; `<name>.wav` there overrides the synthesized placeholder (shipped: bang, shot, reload, move, capture, check; sources in `public/sounds/CREDITS.md`).
- Duration is clamped to 3000 ms. Any click or Esc skips the cinematic. Author at a comfortable pace: the player runs every `at`, `ms` and duration at `CINE_SPEED` (0.6, in `CinematicLayer.tsx`), so a 1500 ms gag plays in 0.9 s.
- Offsets: add +x,y (in squares, y grows downward) to any anchor, for example "victim+0,-0.8" is a little above the victim.
- chance: optional 0..1, the odds that the gag plays once it is picked (the shipped check gag uses 0.5 so it does not nag).
- Validation: a gag with a problem is dropped and reported in the "Content problem" banner. `ms`, `deg`, `size` and `duration` must be numbers (`ms` and `size` not negative), and match fields are checked too: `attacker` and `victim` must be piece kinds or `*`, colours must be `w` or `b`, and `variant` must be a registered variant id.

### Gags for events, not just captures

Set match.event to castling, promotion, check, checkmate or stalemate (no event means capture). Who is who:

- castling: attacker is the king, victim is the rook. Both are on their final squares.
- promotion: attacker is the new piece, victim is the pawn it was. match.attacker "n" targets promotions to a knight.
- check, checkmate, stalemate: attacker is the piece that just moved, victim is the king in trouble.
- capture with match.enPassant true: only en passant captures. It outranks every other capture gag.

Capture gags play on the board as it was before the move. Event gags play on the final position. If one move has both (for example Qxf7#), the capture gag plays first and the event gag follows; a click or Esc skips both. Sounds and memes for that move wait until the gags end.

Shipped sprites for gags: hand-gun.png (supplied art), and duck, crown, sparkle, white-flag, exclamation, tombstone (generated by tools/gen-art.mjs).

### Gags for moments (forks, pins, blunders...)

Set `match.event` to `moment` and `match.moment` to a moment id or a list (`pawn_fork`, `knight_fork`, `fork`, `double_attack`, `pin`, `skewer`, `discovered_attack`, `trapped_piece`, `missed_mate`, `hung_queen`, `hung_piece`). The gag plays on the final position. `sq1`..`sq4` anchor and target the moment's squares:

- forks: sq1 the forking piece, sq2, sq3... its targets (most valuable first, the king first). `attacker` = sq1, `victim` = sq2.
- double_attack: sq1 attacker one, sq2 its target, sq3 attacker two, sq4 its target.
- pin and skewer: sq1 the slider, sq2 the piece in front, sq3 the piece behind.
- discovered_attack: sq1 the piece whose line opened, sq2 what it hits, sq3 the piece that moved. For a discovered check that also hits something: sq1 the piece that moved, sq2 what it hits, sq3 the piece giving check.
- trapped_piece, hung_queen, hung_piece: sq1 the piece that can take, sq2 the piece that is lost.
- missed_mate: sq1 the piece that could have mated, sq2 the mating square, sq3 the king that got away.

`match.victim` on a moment gag matches any involved piece after sq1, so `"victim": "k"` on a fork gag means a king is among the forked pieces. One move gets at most one event gag, chosen in this order: checkmate or stalemate, promotion, castling, then the moments by priority (missed_mate, hung_queen, pawn_fork, trapped_piece, hung_piece, skewer, fork, double_attack, knight_fork, discovered_attack, pin), then check. A gag that fails its `chance` falls through to the next candidate. Only one gun per move: when a capture gag already pulled the hand, gags with `aim` or the hand sprite are skipped, so every moment id ships a caption-only fallback too. A moment a gag told gets no reaction GIF on top. There is no moment for a mate threat on purpose: telling it would warn the defender.

### Aim mode

A gun hand flashes (0.4 s) from each attacker at a piece of the side to move that is obviously hanging: attacked by something cheaper, or attacked with nothing covering it. Subtle losses (a pinned defender, a battery, a long exchange) get no hand, and a mate threat is never shown: the hands are a gag, not coaching. Each threat is shown once; a gag that pointed hands at pieces counts as their warning.

## A new meme: `public/memes/memes.json`

```json
{ "id": "bruh", "on": "blunder", "media": "bruh.gif", "ms": 3000, "sound": "bruh", "caption": "bruh" }
```

Drop the file in `public/memes/`. `on` is any event type (`checkmate`, `check`, `capture`, `promotion`, `castling`, `duckPlaced`, `draw`, `stalemate`, `blunder`, `gameOver`) or a list. Images, GIFs and mp4/webm are supported. 2 to 4 s on screen, with a queue, cooldown and chance set in Settings. `"placeholder": true` shows a PLACEHOLDER tag.

## Online GIFs: `public/memes/gifs.json`

Memes react to what a move did, not just to its type. The engine tags each move with moments: `hung_queen`, `hung_piece`, `queen_lost`, `queen_trade`, `free_piece`, `free_pawn`, `capture_spree`, `check_spam`, `king_walk`, `comeback`, `steamroll`, `underpromotion`, `bongcloud`, `missed_en_passant`, `knight_fork`, `smothered_mate`, `pawn_fork`, `fork`, `double_attack`, `pin`, `skewer`, `discovered_attack`, `trapped_piece`, `missed_mate`, `long_shot` (see `src/engine/moments.ts` and `tactics.ts`). One move can fire several events; only the highest-priority key shows a meme. `long_shot` (a bishop or dragon capture from 4+ squares away) sits on the square the bishop shot from and is marked `sure`: a `sure` key ignores the meme frequency slider, so the sniper shows whenever memes are on.

```json
"hung_queen": { "priority": 85, "chance": 1, "queries": ["facepalm", "oh no"], "refine": [{ "attacker": "p", "queries": ["david and goliath"] }] }
```

- Keys are moments or event types (`checkmate`, `stalemate`, `draw`, `promotion`, `castling`, `check`, `capture`, `duckPlaced`).
- The reaction plays as a flash on the square the moment is about (the capture square, the loose piece, the checked king): no text, played once (short clips hold their last frame to about 0.9 s), at most 2.2 s.
- `queries` are GIPHY search phrases, one picked at random. `refine` entries narrow the search by who did it to whom (`attacker`, `victim`: `p n b r q k d`, a list or `*`); the most specific match wins.
- `chance` is multiplied by the meme frequency setting. Small moments are skipped while another meme is showing or cooling down; priority 90 and up can wait in the queue.
- `rating` (g, pg, pg-13) filters GIPHY content. `blocked` lists GIPHY ids that must never show.
- Needs `VITE_GIPHY_KEY` in `.env` (see `.env.example`) and the "Online GIFs" setting. Without it, or when GIPHY fails or rate-limits, local `memes.json` entries with the same key are used. A local entry's `on` can also name a moment.
- GIPHY rules: searched live from the browser, nothing cached or stored, URLs used as returned, "Powered by GIPHY" plus the creator shown on every GIF. A free beta key allows 100 calls per hour; a public launch needs a production key.

## A new piece set: `public/piecesets/<id>/`

1. Add `<id>` to `public/piecesets/index.json`.
2. Add `set.json` and the images:

```json
{
  "id": "mine", "name": "My Pack", "extends": "classic",
  "pieces": {
    "wn": { "img": "wn.svg", "poses": { "chad": "wn-chad.png" } },
    "bn": { "img": "bn.svg", "poses": { "chad": "bn-chad.png" } }
  }
}
```

Keys are color + kind: `wp wn wb wr wq wk wd` and `bp bn bb br bq bk bd` (`d` = Dragon Bishop). `extends` fills in every piece you do not define. Poses are optional. `smug` shows on the capturing piece, `shocked` on a king in check and on a victim during a cinematic, `chad` on a knight that has captured twice (the Chad Horse rampage; it keeps the pose for the rest of the game).

## Placeholder art

`node tools/gen-art.mjs` regenerates the shipped placeholders (Classic set, duck, meme cards). `piecesets/chad` is hand-maintained: Classic knights plus the Chad Horse rampage pose. The hand-and-gun sprite is supplied art. Replace any file with final art.
