Instructions

Standard Chess: Move pieces according to traditional chess rules.

Fischer Random: Starting position is randomized with bishops on opposite colors and king between rooks.

Dragon Bishop: Bishops move like standard bishops but can also jump like a Xiangqi horse (one step orthogonally then one step diagonally out).

Duck Chess: After each move, place the duck on any empty square to block movement through that square.

Chess Rules

Check

When a king is under direct attack. Player must move to remove the threat.

Checkmate

When a king is in check with no legal moves to escape. Game ends immediately.

Pin

A piece cannot move off the line between it and its king, shielding it from attack.

En Passant

Special pawn capture when opponent moves two squares from starting position.
